PIC 101 · CHED-Aligned Course

Professional Issuesin Computing

A teaching-ready course organized as four connected whole-topic units. Each session works as a focused subtopic that develops the knowledge and professional judgment needed for the unit.

Course Materials

How the topics are arranged: Begin with the whole topic for each unit, then teach its session-level subtopics in sequence. Open any lesson card to start its full-screen, offline presentation.
Unit 1 · Prelim

Professional Foundations and Ethical Reasoning

Build the foundation for understanding computing as a profession, using ethical principles, stakeholder analysis, due care, and professional codes to make responsible decisions.

Weeks 1–4
TopicWeek 1 · Session 1

Computing as a Profession and Public Responsibility

Define professional computing work and explain why technical decisions carry obligations to people and society.

→ TopicWeek 1 · Session 2

History of Computing, Society, and Professional Practice

Connect major developments in computing with changing professional responsibilities and social expectations.

→ TopicWeek 2 · Session 1

Professional Roles, Organizations, and Codes of Ethics

Distinguish computing roles and examine how professional organizations and ethical codes guide practice.

→ TopicWeek 2 · Session 2

Professional Conduct, Competence, and Lifelong Learning

Apply competence, integrity, continuous learning, and responsible conduct to workplace situations.

→ TopicWeek 3 · Session 1

Ethical Theories and Structured Ethical Reasoning

Use consequence-, duty-, rights-, and character-based reasoning to examine computing dilemmas.

→ TopicWeek 3 · Session 2

Stakeholders, Harm, Public Interest, and Accountability

Identify affected groups, compare risks and benefits, and defend decisions in the public interest.

→ TopicWeek 4 · Session 1

Professional Codes: Principles and Responsibilities

Translate broad professional principles into specific responsibilities and safeguards.

→ TopicWeek 4 · Session 2

Ethical Case Analysis and Defensible Decisions

Integrate facts, stakeholders, duties, options, and safeguards into a defensible recommendation.

→
Unit 2 · Midterm

Computing in Society, Privacy, Cybercrime, and Intellectual Property

Examine how computing reshapes society and work, then apply Philippine privacy, cybercrime, and intellectual-property principles to realistic professional cases.

Weeks 5–9
TopicWeek 5 · Session 1

Social Impact of Computing: Benefits, Risks, and Trade-offs

Evaluate how technologies distribute benefits, risks, power, and unintended consequences.

→ TopicWeek 5 · Session 2

Digital Citizenship, Online Conduct, and Expression

Practice responsible participation, communication, and expression in digital spaces.

→ TopicWeek 6 · Session 1

Technology, Work, Automation, and Organizational Change

Analyze how automation changes tasks, jobs, skills, accountability, and organizational decisions.

→ TopicWeek 6 · Session 2

Digital Divide, Inclusion, and Accessibility

Assess barriers to access and design more inclusive and accessible digital experiences.

→ TopicWeek 7 · Session 1

Philippine Data Privacy Foundations

Explain lawful processing, data-subject rights, accountability, and core privacy principles.

→ TopicWeek 7 · Session 2

Privacy by Design, Rights, and Breach Response

Build privacy into systems and respond responsibly when personal data is exposed.

→ TopicWeek 8 · Session 1

Cybercrime Law and Computer-Related Offenses

Recognize computer-related offenses and connect legal boundaries with professional conduct.

→ TopicWeek 8 · Session 2

Digital Evidence, Reporting, and Due Process

Preserve evidence, document incidents, report properly, and respect fair procedures.

→ TopicWeek 9 · Session 1

Intellectual Property in Computing

Differentiate copyright, patent, trademark, trade secret, and ownership issues in technology work.

→ TopicWeek 9 · Session 2

Software Licensing, Open Source, Piracy, and Fair Use

Interpret licenses and make responsible choices when reusing software and digital content.

→
Unit 3 · Semifinals

Workplace Policy, Security, Quality, Governance, and Emerging Technology

Connect professional rules with secure and reliable practice, then evaluate governance, organizational risk, responsible AI, surveillance, and other emerging technologies.

Weeks 10–14
TopicWeek 10 · Session 1

Contracts, Acceptable Use, and Workplace Policy

Interpret professional agreements, workplace rules, authorization, and acceptable-use boundaries.

→ TopicWeek 10 · Session 2

Confidentiality, Records, and Responsible Data Handling

Classify, protect, retain, share, and dispose of workplace information responsibly.

→ TopicWeek 11 · Session 1

Security as a Professional and Social Responsibility

Treat security as a continuing duty involving people, process, technology, and public trust.

→ TopicWeek 11 · Session 2

Human Factors, Social Engineering, and Security Awareness

Recognize manipulation tactics and design awareness practices that reduce human-centered risk.

→ TopicWeek 12 · Session 1

Software Quality, Reliability, Safety, and Testing

Relate quality assurance and testing to foreseeable harm, reliability, and professional care.

→ TopicWeek 12 · Session 2

Project Responsibility, Documentation, and Change Control

Use documentation, review, traceability, and controlled change to maintain accountability.

→ TopicWeek 13 · Session 1

Computing Governance, Audit, and Compliance

Explain how policies, controls, evidence, and oversight guide responsible technology use.

→ TopicWeek 13 · Session 2

Organizational Risk, Continuity, and Incident Ethics

Prepare for disruption and balance rapid incident response with rights, transparency, and fairness.

→ TopicWeek 14 · Session 1

Responsible AI: Bias, Transparency, and Oversight

Evaluate AI systems for bias, explainability, human control, accountability, and affected rights.

→ TopicWeek 14 · Session 2

IoT, Biometrics, Surveillance, and Emerging Technology

Assess proportionality, consent, security, and social impact in connected and monitoring technologies.

→
Unit 4 · Finals

Digital Responsibility, Professional Practice, and Integration

Apply professional responsibility to information integrity, sustainability, communication, teamwork, career development, and an integrated computing case.

Weeks 15–18
TopicWeek 15 · Session 1

Misinformation, Manipulation, and Platform Responsibility

Examine deceptive content, algorithmic amplification, user responsibility, and platform duties.

→ TopicWeek 15 · Session 2

Sustainability, E-Waste, and Responsible IT

Connect computing choices with energy use, product life cycles, e-waste, and sustainable practice.

→ TopicWeek 16 · Session 1

Professional Communication and Public Risk Communication

Communicate technical findings, uncertainty, incidents, and risks clearly to different audiences.

→ TopicWeek 16 · Session 2

Teamwork, Conflict, Diversity, and Psychological Safety

Build respectful teams that surface concerns, manage disagreement, and improve decisions.

→ TopicWeek 17 · Session 1

Professional Development and Lifelong Learning

Create a realistic development plan based on changing tools, roles, standards, and responsibilities.

→ TopicWeek 17 · Session 2

Ethical Resume, Portfolio, Job Search, and Interview Practice

Represent skills and experience honestly while protecting confidential and proprietary information.

→ TopicWeek 18 · Session 1

Integrated Professional Issues Case Analysis

Combine ethical, legal, social, security, and professional reasoning in one complex case.

→ TopicWeek 18 · Session 2

Capstone Ethical Position and Professional Development Plan

Present a reasoned professional position and a practical plan for continued ethical growth.
